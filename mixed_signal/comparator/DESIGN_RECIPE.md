# Design recipe: comparator, the short-channel comparator of the 8-bit bottom-plate cell

Date: 2026-10-06 (verification 2026-10-07). Topology unchanged from [`../comparator_bkp/comparator_updt_v2`](../comparator_bkp/comparator_updt_v2/DESIGN_RECIPE.md):
a PMOS 5T OTA, a starved inverter and an output inverter, 10 devices. The
interactive Pareto report is `assets/comparator_pareto.html`, generated by
`report_data.py`.

Labels: **user** (given), **contract** (from `../Analog_top_bkp/unit_cell_bps/CONTRACT.md`
or `../comparator_bkp/comparator_updt/SPEC.md`), **PDK** (tables, model cards, DRC deck),
**measured** (ngspice; the run is named), **assumed** (a judgement).

## 2. Device data (lut/)

- **Short-L gm/Id tables.** The repo table has only L = 0.28, 0.29 and 0.30 um
  below 0.4 um. `lut/build.sh` re-runs the techsweep at L = 0.28, 0.30, 0.32,
  0.34 and 0.36 um with W = 2 um, nf = 1. It uses the repo's deck and
  `build_lut.py` with only the L grid and the paths changed.
- **Device noise.** The tables carry no noise. `lut/noise_char.spice` runs
  ngspice `.noise` on one W = 2 um device of each flavour at every L, VGS
  0.45-1.80 V, VDS 1 V, one point per decade from 1 kHz to 1 GHz
  (`data/device_noise.csv`). Per (flavour, L, gm/Id), the gate-referred
  density is fitted as S(f) = A (f/1k)^-af + B, where:
  - A and af come from 1k/10k (af 0.95-1.1);
  - B comes from 100 MHz minus the flicker tail.
  - At 1 GHz C_gd feed-through corrupts low-gm points, so 1 GHz is not used.
  - The model uses A/(W L) and gamma = B gm / 4kT.

## 3. Search (size_v3.py, stage A, tables only)

**Variables.**

- Lengths: L_in, L_ld (load and buffers), L_b (tail and starve), each in
  {0.28, 0.30, 0.32, 0.34, 0.36} um.
- Finger widths: W_fp and W_fn, each in {0.5, 1, 2} um.
- Finger counts: nf_in 1-64, nf_ld 1-48, nf_tail and nf_st 1-100.
- Current per bias finger: i_f, continuous.

The uniform samples draw gm/Id targets, which are rounded to whole fingers:
bias 5-20, pair 8-24, load 4-18 S/A. gm/Id is then re-derived from
(W, L, I).

**Per design:**

1. **Bias and tail.**
   - The reference diode's VSG comes from J_D = i_f / W_fp.
   - The tail current is taken at the tail's own VDS. This is a short-L
     mirror, and its VDS error is kept: -7 % in the selected design.
   - The starve current is taken at VDS = 1 V (assumed).
2. **Operating point.** As v2's model: V_left, V_tail, the pair's body
   effect, and the trip of XBUF1 (= XLOADM) at I_starve.
3. **Small signal.**
   - OTA gain, starved-stage gain and inverter gain.
   - C_outa and C_outb from the tables.
   - tau = C_outa / g_out.
   - alpha = C_S / (C_S + C_gg,pair + 1.63 fF).
4. **Noise.**
   - S_in = 2 S_pair + 2 (gm_ld/gm_in)^2 S_load.
   - Thermal is integrated over the pole's ENBW (pi/2 f_p).
   - 1/f is integrated from 1 kHz to f_p, closed form.
5. **Mismatch, pedestal, dead zone, headroom.** v2's formulas, with the
   PDK `fets_mm` parameters. L_eff = L - 0.15 um, which is what makes short
   L expensive in offset.
6. **Delay and fall: a transient on the tables.** v2's closed-form one-pole
   model was a screen; here it read 2.4x slow on delay and up to 2x fast on
   the fall. It is replaced, for designs that pass every static constraint,
   by a four-node backward-Euler transient on the tables' I-V curves:
   - **outa:** C_outa and g_out, driven by -I_tail tanh(gm v_d / I_tail),
     with v_d = alpha x slope x t.
   - **bst:** XBST (gate at the reference VSG, finite r_o) into XBUF2.
   - **outb:** XBUF2 against XBUF1.
   - **dout:** XBUF4 against XBUF3 into 10 fF + drains.
   - **Couplings:** C_gd of XBUF1/2 (outa-outb) and XBUF3/4 (outb-dout).
   - **Static trip:** the DC point where outb sits at the inverter
     threshold.
   - **Step:** 50 ps; a 20 ps step gave the same numbers.

   Two simplifications were each shown wrong by ngspice and replaced:
   - an ideal-current pull-up on outb gave infinite starved-stage gain and
     fall times 0.6x ngspice;
   - leaving out Miller coupling gave a further 0.7x.
7. **Calibration** (`PHASE=CAL`, `data/calibration.csv`/`.json`). 38 stage-A
   designs, stratified over log power, with modelled delay and fall
   0.3-3x the gates, were timed in ngspice (plateau level and the noise
   bench). ngspice / model:

   | quantity | median | p10-p90 | used as |
   | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
   | delay from the trip | 0.975 | 0.68-1.16 | factor 0.975 |
   | dout fall | 1.40 | 0.99-2.62 | exp(0.399 + 4.363 (L_ld - 0.32 um)): 1.25x at 0.28, 1.77x at 0.36 um (log residual 0.33) |
   | noise at the pins | 1.05 | 0.94-1.16 | not applied; 7x margin |

**Run** (`PHASE=B SIGMA_GATE_LSB=99 PARETO_POINTS=120000 LOCAL_POINTS=30000 ROUNDS=4 N_FRONT_B=8`, seed 20261006):

- **Stage A:** 120 000 uniform samples, then 4 local rounds of 30 000
  around the front. 17 designs were feasible after the uniform sample and
  24 753 after round 4.
- **Noise was never binding.** The worst feasible design reads 0.54 mV in
  the model.
- **Delay and fall bind,** and they set the current.

## 6. Selection: f003, the knee at sigma <= 2 LSB

`PHASE=SELECT SELECT_SIGMA=2 SIZING_EMIT=1` takes the selection from the
recorded stage B.

**Why f003.**

- **It meets every user gate in ngspice.**
- **sigma(Vos) is the one 8-bit criterion that cannot be met.** f003 is the
  knee of the lowest cap that has ngspice-passing designs.
- **The in-cell spread is the risk.** In the assembled v2 cell the offset
  spread was 1.8x the bench value, 2.38 vs 1.29 LSB, through Miller
  multiplication on the floating top plate.
- **3 sigma must fit the cell's margins.** A cell at -3 sigma trips early.
  The ramp pre-roll must cover 3 sigma, and pedestal + 3 sigma must stay
  inside the 19-count overrun.
- **The cost.** f003 spends 20.6 uW and 8.7 um2 more than f008 for 0.45 LSB
  less spread.
- **When f008 is better.** If the in-cell Monte Carlo of f003 shows little
  Miller multiplication, f008 is the cheaper choice.

| Device | Fingers x W_f, L | Current (design) | gm/Id (ngspice) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| XINP / XINN (pair) | 12 x 1 um, L 0.32 um | 8.5 uA each | 14.9 S/A |
| XLOADD / XLOADM | 1 x 0.5 um, L 0.36 um | 8.5 uA each | 5.6 S/A |
| XTAIL | 11 x 1 um, L 0.28 um | 17 uA | 12.1 S/A |
| XBST | 5 x 1 um, L 0.28 um | 9.5 uA | 10.7 S/A |
| XBUF1 / XBUF3 | = XLOADM | | |
| XBUF2 / XBUF4 | 1 x 1 um, L 0.36 um | | |
| reference (bench) | 1 x 1 um, L 0.28 um, I_B = 1.93 uA (J_D 1.93 uA/um) | | |

Currents at I_B = 1.93 uA, typical, at the trip point (`intent.py`, 0.5 uA
steps), checked against the drawn netlist by `intent_op.py` within 5 %
(`make comparator-schematic`); gm/Id from the same ngspice op. The
sizing model's gm/Id-table estimate (`data/sizing.json`: tail 19.73, pair
9.86 uA, gm/Id 10.9 / 14.0 / 5.2 / 10.9) was 15 % high for the tail and the
pair: the circuit settles at a lower tail V_SD than the model took, so the 11
tail fingers copy ~8.8 x I_B. Every ngspice record of this design ran at the
currents in the table. Gain at the trip point: OTA 45 x starved inverter 56 x
output inverter 29 = 6.9e4 V/V (97 dB) inn -> dout.

Other figures:

- **Operating point.** alpha 0.761, OTA gain 44.5 V/V with its pole at
  22.3 MHz (ngspice). Tail headroom margin 0.71 V.
- **Pedestal (model).** 1.67 LSB; pedestal + 3 sigma = 7.6 counts.
- **Power.** 96.9 uW in the worst static state by the sizing model. ngspice on
  the drawing (`intent_op.py`, I_B 1.93 uA, diode included): 112 uW at the
  trip point (both inverters conduct), 62-63 uW converting (dout settled). At
  the Analog_top bias (3.25 uA): 283 uW at the trip point, 108-110 uW
  converting; the trip-point crowbar depends on the cell's offset (157 / 112 uW
  at -/+1 sigma). The mean over a conversion is 58-89 uW (ngspice, I_B 1.93 uA).
- **Area.** Gate area 13.6 um2.

## 9. PVT (`./scripts/sim.sh comparator-pvt`, records `runs/comparator-updt-v3-pvt/20261007T024232Z`, `-pvt-geom/20261007T024803Z`)

**Grid.** typical / ss / ff / sf / fs x 0 / 27 / 85 C x 3.0 / 3.3 / 3.6 V,
which is 45 cells. inp = VBL = VDD/2.

**Bias.** The ideal current is held constant over PVT (user: the bias
generator comes later); the reference finger follows the corner.

**Benches.** At every cell: `tb_sel_dc` + `tb_sel` at 4.5 and 40.5 LSB, and
`tb_nz` (`pvt_v3.py`, `data/pvt.csv`). The grid was repeated with the drawn
S/D geometry (`DUT_GEOM=1`, `data/pvt_geom.csv`).

| Gate | Controlled deck | Drawn S/D geometry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| delay from the trip <= 12.5 ns | **42/45**; 8.72-12.75 ns; misses at ss/85 C (12.62 / 12.68 / 12.75 ns at 3.0 / 3.3 / 3.6 V) | **36/45**; 9.30-13.56 ns; misses at all nine ss cells (12.63-13.56 ns) |
| level spread <= 5 ns | 45/45 (max 0.00 ns) | 45/45 |
| dout fall <= 6 ns | **29/45**; worst 10.38 ns at ff/85/3.6. Misses at hot, high supply and ff: ff/85/3.6 10.4, ff/85/3.3 8.4, ff/27/3.6 7.9, fs/85/3.6 7.8, typical/85/3.6 7.4 ns | **27/45**; worst 10.44 ns |
| armed at TR | 45/45 | 45/45 |
| noise at the pins < 1.2 mV | 45/45; max 0.189 mV (fs/85/3.6) | 45/45; max 0.184 mV |
| mean power over a conversion | <= 98 uW | |

**Why the fall misses.** The fall is quasi-static. dout follows outa,
which the attenuated ramp moves at A x alpha x 117 mV/us, about 4 mV/ns,
through the starved stage's finite gain. Both the OTA gain and the
starved-stage gain drop at ff and high temperature (OTA 37.6 V/V at
ff/85/3.6, against 44.5 typical).

**What the 6 ns limit is.** It is the slowest d edge `../latch_bkp/cmp_tspc` was
verified with, not a measured failure of the latch. A slower edge needs
either the latch re-verified, or a design with fall margin.

**Why the delay misses.** At ss/85 the delay grows by about 2 ns at the
same current; the bias is ideal and fixed in this grid.

## 11. VACASK transient noise (`trannoise.py`, `vacask.sh`, record `runs/comparator-updt-v3-vacask/20261007T024958Z`)

conversion sees.

Engine and measurement checks (typical):

- PSD of the transient record against the noise analysis: x1.018 over
  100 kHz-20 MHz, x1.008 over 1-300 kHz.
- Numerical floor (noise off, same grid): 2.6e-6 of the noisy sigma.
- noisefmax 1 GHz against 200 MHz: x1.006.
- Every conversion crossed.
- Against the ngspice small-signal figure (0.168 mV over the own band, s.7)

### 12.1 The offset is signal-independent (`offset_lin.py`, `./scripts/sim.sh comparator-pvt PHASES=lin`, record `runs/comparator-updt-v3-lin/20261007T205548Z`)

**Method.**

- **Samples.** 30 ngspice Monte Carlo samples per cell, at typical / 27 /
  3.3, ss / 85 / 3.0 and ff / 85 / 3.6.
- **Levels.** Each sample runs at four levels, 4.5 / 40.5 / 128.5 / 250.5
  LSB, in the cell's charge path (`tb_sel.spice`: the 54.52 fF MIM, ideal
  S2, the 8-bit ramp, 10 fF).
- **The same draw at every level.** The mismatch draw is identical at all
  four levels: `.options seed=N` per sample, in the same netlist.
- **The figure.** The code offset of a sample at a level is
  t(dout falls) - t(top crosses VBL), in counts.
- **The window** runs to 28 counts past the ideal crossing.
- **Gate.** Per sample, the offset moves <= 0.1 LSB between levels (SPEC
  item 10).

| cell | largest level dependence of any sample | mean | nominal (no mismatch) | gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| typical / 27 / 3.3 | 0.0002 LSB | 0.0001 | 0.0001 | PASS (30/30) |
| ss / 85 / 3.0 | 0.0002 LSB | 0.0001 | 0.0002 | PASS (30/30) |
| ff / 85 / 3.6 | 0.0017 LSB | 0.0001 | 0.0001 | PASS (30/30) |

**What the calibration has to absorb.** The in-cell code offset is larger
than the bench's sigma(Vos), and it is skewed late:

| cell | mean | sigma | range over 30 samples |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| typical / 27 / 3.3 | +1.98 LSB | 4.15 LSB | -4.52 .. +11.34 |
| ss / 85 / 3.0 | +2.13 | 4.23 | -4.40 .. +11.48 |
| ff / 85 / 3.6 | +1.77 | 3.81 | -4.11 .. +10.34 |

**Why it is larger.** The bench's sigma(Vos) (about 2.1 LSB) is
multiplied, as in v2, by the pair's C_gd on the floating top plate as outa
swings: 2.38 against 1.29 LSB there, about x2 here. Statistically, sigma
from 30 samples carries +-13 %.

**What it requires.**

- **Ramp pre-roll >= ~11 LSB.** Mean - 3 sigma is about -10.5 LSB. The
  earlier 7 LSB recommendation is no longer enough. Two samples at typical
  and two at ss were already tripped at 4.5 LSB (offsets -4.4 to -4.5 LSB);
  that is the dead zone the pre-roll removes.
- **Overrun.** Mean + 3 sigma is about +14.8 counts, against the 19-count
  overrun, so it is inside.
- **Calibration range.** The pedestal calibration must cover about -11 to
  +15 LSB.

### 12.2 Bias-generator requirement (`bias_req.py`, record `runs/comparator-updt-v3-lin-bias/20261007T205146Z`)

**Method.** At each of the 45 PVT cells, the ideal bias was swept from
0.8x to 1.4x of the nominal 1.931 uA per finger; tail and starve scale
together. The requirement is the smallest scale from which the delay from
the trip is <= 12.5 ns at 4.5 and 40.5 LSB (`data/bias_req.csv`).

| | controlled deck | with the drawn S/D geometry (closer to layout) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cells needing more than 1.0x | ss / 85 / {3.0, 3.3, 3.6}: 1.05x | ss / 0 / *: 1.05x; ss / 27 / *: 1.10x; ss / 85 / *: 1.20x |
| cells meeting 12.5 ns already at 0.8x | 33 / 45 | 18 / 45 |
| worst requirement | **1.05x at ss / 85** | **1.20x at ss / 85** |

**Requirement on the generator.** It must deliver at least 1.20x nominal
at ss / 85 C and 1.10x at ss / 27 C. Equivalently, at fixed nominal
current, the comparator lacks 0.25-1.1 ns at the ss corner.

**Two cautions.**

- **A typical-corner floor.** A generator whose current falls at ss / hot,
  as the v2 bias_gen's 0.69x end did, misses 12.5 ns: at typical, 0.8x
  gives 11.9 ns, and at ss / 85 / 3.6 it gives 14.3 ns. So the generator
  also has a lower bound, about 0.9x, at the typical corner.
- **The other knobs.** A higher current also speeds the fall (s.9) and
  costs power (about +20 % at 1.2x).
