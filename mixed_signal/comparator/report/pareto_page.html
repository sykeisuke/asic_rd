8-bit bottom-plate ADC · comparator · GF180MCU 3.3 V · 2026-10-06

cmp_updt_v3: short-channel comparator Pareto

The 10-device comparator (PMOS 5T OTA, starved inverter, output inverter) re-sized with every L ≤ 0.36 µm. Delay from its own trip point must stay under ¼ count (12.5 ns at 20 MHz). It drives 10 fF, and its noise at the pins stays under 1.2 mV. Power and area are minimised. A gm/Id table search proposes the designs, ngspice decides, and the chosen point is marked in every chart.

Selected design · f003 · typical corner

Accepted for the cell, under three decisions (ADR 0015, 2026-10-07)

The tiles below are the typical corner, where every gate you set is met. Over the 45 PVT cells noise passes everywhere (VACASK transient noise 0.036–0.038 LSB). The three departures from the contract are now decided:

  • The offset spread is accepted. It is 1.8–2.2 LSB on the bench (3.8–4.2 LSB in the cell), above the 4/3 LSB contract. The condition was that it be signal-independent, and it is: each mismatched cell's offset moves at most 0.0017 LSB from 4.5 to 250.5 LSB.
  • The dout fall over PVT is waived. It exceeds 6 ns at 16 cells, up to 10.4 ns.
  • The delay at ss goes to the bias generator. At ss / 85 °C it needs at least 1.20× the nominal current (1.05× without the drawn S/D geometry).

Pareto front 2

Offset spread costs power, and 4/3 LSB is out of reach

With L ≤ 0.36 µm the PDK mismatch model's effective length is L − 0.15 µm, so devices match poorly per unit area. Most of the spread comes from the load mirror, which "buffer NMOS = XLOADM" ties to the speed-critical output nodes. The lowest spread any feasible design reaches is about 1.6 LSB, and only at about 180 µW.

Why this point

Choosing between the two knees

No design meets the contract's 4/3 LSB. That leaves two knees that pass every ngspice gate:

f003 is selected, the knee of the σ ≤ 2 LSB front. It costs 21 µW and 9 µm² more than f008 and keeps 0.45 LSB less offset spread. For the 8-bit ADC this matters in the cell, not on this bench. In v2 the in-cell offset spread came out 1.8× the bench value (2.38 vs 1.29 LSB) through Miller multiplication on the floating top plate. A cell at −3σ trips early, so the ramp pre-roll has to cover 3σ, and pedestal + 3σ must stay inside the 19-count overrun window. f003 leaves more of that window free. If the in-cell Monte Carlo comes back small, f008 is the cheaper choice.

Decision 1 · offset

The offset is a per-cell constant, so calibration removes it

30 Monte Carlo samples at each of three cells. Each sample's mismatch draw is identical at four ramp levels, and it runs in the cell's charge path: the 54.52 fF MIM, ideal S2, the 8-bit ramp, 10 fF on dout. Each line below is one sample's code offset against the level. They are flat: no sample moves by more than 0.0017 LSB. A sample whose line starts at 40.5 LSB was already tripped at 4.5 LSB. Its offset is below −4.5 LSB, and the ramp pre-roll covers it.

The calibration and the ramp timing carry the cost. The in-cell offset is +2 ± 4 LSB, skewed late, from −4.5 to +11.5 LSB. That calls for a ramp pre-roll of at least about 11 LSB (the earlier 7 LSB is not enough) and a calibration span of about −11 to +15 LSB. Mean + 3σ is about 15 counts, inside the 19-count overrun.

Decision 3 · bias generator

What the comparator's bias generator must deliver

At each of the 45 PVT cells the ideal bias was swept from 0.8× to 1.4× of 1.931 µA per finger, with tail and starve together. The table lists the smallest current at which the delay from the trip is within 12.5 ns at 4.5 and 40.5 LSB.

Verification

ngspice record of the selected design

Typical corner, 27 °C, 3.3 V, ideal bias current into one diode-connected tail finger. Record: (./scripts/sim.sh comparator-updt-v3).

Devices

Sizes, and the gm/Id each device lands at

One finger width per flavour (PMOS 1 µm, NMOS 0.5 µm). Every size is an integer count of fingers. gm/Id follows from W, L and I through the short-L tables; it is not a free variable.

Method

How the fronts were found before any simulation, and how far to trust them

  1. Built gm/Id tables at L = 0.28, 0.30, 0.32, 0.34 and 0.36 µm (the repo's table only had 0.28–0.30 below 0.4 µm), plus single-device ngspice noise at the same L for thermal and 1/f, which the tables lack.
  2. Random search over 120 000 designs, then 4 local rounds of 30 000. Variables: three L (pair, load and buffers, bias), the two finger widths, four finger counts, and the bias finger's current density J_D.
  3. Each design gets an operating point, gain, poles, noise (1 kHz to its own pole), Pelgrom offset, pedestal, dead zone and headroom from the tables. A four-node transient on the tables' I-V curves (outa, bst, outb, dout, with the buffers' Miller capacitances) then gives the delay and the dout fall.
  4. That transient was calibrated against ngspice on 38 stratified designs. Delay: median ratio 0.98. Noise: 1.05. Fall: 1.25× at L 0.28 µm to 1.77× at 0.36 µm, fitted in L.
  5. The fronts at each offset cap were timed in ngspice: 13 of 14 points passed, and the one failure missed by 0.03 ns.
Assumptions

What was assumed, and why

  • Delay is measured from the instant the top plate crosses the comparator's own DC trip to dout at 50 % (user choice). The VBL-referenced delay, which includes the offset, is reported.
  • Noise is input-referred at the pins: thermal + 1/f from 1 kHz, integrated over the OTA's own pole (user choice). 1.2 mV matches the old spec's 0.25 LSB at the ADC input.
  • 10 fF is the whole dout load (latch + wire). Wire on outa and outb is assumed at 5 fF and 2 fF until extraction.
  • Bias comes from an ideal current into one tail finger. The bias generator's 0.69× and 1.77× PVT ends are reported, not gated. At 0.69× the delay is 12.97 ns.
  • Kept from v2's contract: sigma(Vos) ≤ 4/3 LSB (marked "assumed" there, and reported here as unmet), pedestal ≤ 8 LSB, pedestal + 3σ ≤ 19 counts, dead zone ≤ 1 LSB, dout fall ≤ 6 ns (the latch's verified edge), and 0.2 V of tail headroom at 1× and 1.77× bias and at VDD = 3.0 V.
  • Area is the device footprint. Each finger carries a contacted S/D strip of 0.52 µm, or 0.44 µm at the ends (PDK DRC CO.1, CO.4, CO.7). Routing is not included.
  • Typical corner only. PVT and in-cell mismatch are not run yet.

Transient noise

VACASK transient noise under the 8-bit ramp

Every device noise source injects in the time domain while the attenuated ramp crosses VBL, repeated over hundreds of conversions in one record. The spread of dout's falling instant, times the ramp slope at the pins, is the noise the conversion sees. The engine is first checked against the small-signal noise analysis at the same bias.
