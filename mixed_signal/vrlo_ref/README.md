# vrlo_ref — the VR_LO supplier for the ramp reset

`ramp_simple` (the ramp in `Analog_top`) holds `vramp` at `vrlo` through its reset
switch XRST while `ramp_rst` is high. Until now nothing on the chip made `vrlo`:
every bench used an ideal 0.5 V source `VVRLO`. This block replaces it with a
shunt regulator on a 5/33 AVDD divider tap.

## 1. What it has to do

| # | Requirement | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Level | 0.5 V at AVDD 3.3 V, ratiometric: 5/33 AVDD | [D] user decision 2026-10-09 (AVDD divider tap, like VREF/VBL) |
| R2 | Sink the ramp current while `ramp_rst` is high | 0.487 uA typical (0.469 uA x 1.038), 0.36-0.79 uA over PVT | [D] `runs/analog-top/20261009T020546Z`; ramp current 0.74-1.62x untrimmed (ADR 0017) |
| R3 | Absorb the end-of-ramp dump | 4 pF x (2.162 - 0.5) V = 6.65 pC each time `ramp_rst` rises | [D] `vramp_at_tovr` 2.1618 V in the same record |
| R4 | Back to the reset level after the dump | within 0.05 LSB (0.293 mV) 1 us after `ramp_rst` rises | [A] TREC = 1 us (7 % of a 13.8 us conversion); 0.05 LSB as R5 |
| R5 | The S1 charge left at TR | the four cells' bottom plates join `vramp` while it is in reset; what is left at TR (full-scale minus empty cells) <= 0.05 LSB | [D] `../ramp_gen/COMPARISON.md` s.4.3: the reference's allocation. A residual that depends on the four inputs is cell-to-cell crosstalk |
| R6 | Timeline | the ramp is released **300 ns** after the last S1 closes (TR >= TS3 + 300 ns; Analog_top's bench uses 100 ns) | [D] user decision 2026-10-10, s.3.3 |
| R7 | Record to record | vramp at TR, record 2 - record 1 (same inputs) <= 0.05 LSB | [D] as R5: the pedestal must be the same every record |
| R8 | Start-up | vramp within 0.05 LSB of its settled value at T0 = 3 us from a cold supply | [D] `Analog_top/tb_params.inc` T0 |
| R9 | Systematic level error | \|vrlo - 5/33 AVDD\| <= 2 LSB (11.7 mV) at every PVT cell | [A] half the ~4 overrun counts left after the in-cell offset mean + 3 sigma (15 of 19 counts, `comparator` record). First set at 1 LSB; s.3.2 |
| R10 | Mismatch | sigma(vrlo) <= 1 LSB | [A] vrlo's offset is common to the four cells and adds to the same overrun margin |
| R11 | Noise | vrlo at the instant TR, 1 Hz - 1 GHz, <= 0.04 LSB | [A] the ramp's share is 0.07 LSB (ADR 0013), the ramp itself measured 0.043: rss 0.059 |
| R12 | Stability | loop phase margin >= 45 deg in the reset state (XRST on, 4 pF on vrlo) and in the ramp state (only the decap) | [A] |
| R13 | Bias span | R4-R12 hold with the ideal bias at 0.69x / 1.77x and the ramp current at 0.74x / 1.62x | [A] the span `midrail_ref_v2` used; [D] ADR 0017 |

The level is a pedestal: the mandatory pedestal calibration (ADR 0013) removes
its offset from every code. What the calibration cannot remove is a residual
that changes with the signal (R5) or from record to record (R4, R7, R11).

**Ratiometric, not absolute.** 5/33 AVDD moves +-45 mV (+-7.7 LSB) over AVDD
3.0-3.6 V. That was accepted with the choice of the divider. It shifts the
bottom of the input range; the calibration absorbs the offset.

## 2. Circuit

```text
 avdd ─┬───────────────┬──────────────┬─────────────┬──────────── avdd
       │               │              │             │
     28 units     XMBP ┤pb─────── XMT ┤ (tail)  XMK ┤ (keep-alive, 3 x IB)
       │               │              │ts           │
  tap ─┼─ XCT          │         ┌────┴────┐        │
       │ (1 pF)        │      XM2┤tap   vrlo├XM1    │
     5 units           │         │g        │d1      │
       │        ib─XMBN│XMBN2    ├── XM5   │        ├──────── vrlo
 avss ─┴───────────────┴──────── XM4 ── XM3 (diode) │
                                 g ─────────────── XMS (shunt) ── XCD (3.44 pF)
```

- **Divider.** 28 + 5 identical `ppolyf_u_3k` units (1 x 20 um), so the ratio
  is set by matching: tap = 5/33 AVDD. XCT (1 pF MIM) filters the tap.
- **OTA.** A PMOS input pair at the 0.5 V common mode; an NMOS pair would need
  its tail below ground. XM1 senses vrlo on the diode side and XM2 the tap
  on the output side. The output g carries the diode XM5 in parallel with the
  mirror XM4 (N3 = N4 + N5). Its resistance is about 1/gm5, which puts the
  OTA pole far above the vrlo pole and makes the gain A ~ N3/N5 = 3.
- **Shunt.** XMS mirrors XM5 (9:1). vrlo up -> XM1 off -> XM2 on -> g up ->
  XMS sinks more. The shunt can sink tens of uA when g slews, which takes the
  6.6 pC dump.
- **Keep-alive.** XMK sources 3 x IB = 4.1 uA from avdd into vrlo. It brings
  vrlo up from a cold supply. It also keeps the shunt alive while the ramp
  runs (no inflow then). The shunt carries the keep-alive plus the ramp
  current in reset.
- **Decap.** XCD (3.44 pF MIM) on vrlo is the loop's dominant pole in both
  states.

| Device | Size | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XR1-XR33 | ppolyf_u_3k 1 x 20 um each | divider 28 : 5 |
| XCT / XCD | MIM 22.4^2 / 41.5^2 um | tap filter 1.0 pF / vrlo decap 3.44 pF |
| XMBN, XMBN2 / XMBP | nfet L 1 W 1 / pfet L 1 W 2 | bias: ib -> pb |
| XMT / XMK | pfet L 1, 2 / 3 fingers of 2 um | tail 2.74 uA / keep-alive 4.1 uA |
| XM1, XM2 | pfet L 0.5, 7 fingers of 2 um | input pair |
| XM3 / XM4 / XM5 | nfet L 1, 6 / 4 / 2 fingers of 1 um | load diode, mirror, g-node diode |
| XMS | nfet L 1, 18 fingers of 1 um | shunt |

Typical, 27 C, 3.3 V, reset state: 11.8 uA from avdd including the ideal
bias, **38.8 uW** [M]. Drawn area **2942 um2**: MOS 58, poly 660, MIM 2224 [P]
(device area only, no spacing or routing).

## 3. How it was sized

`size_vrlo.py` samples the free sizes at random. It derives the keep-alive
so that it balances the shunt's mirror-set current in reset. Stage A judges
each point on `tb_tran.spice` and `tb_loop.spice` at typical and at
ss/85 C/3.0 V. Stage B takes the passers in power x area order and checks
them at the PVT extremes, the spans, under mismatch and for noise. The search
took five rounds. The earlier ones are kept as records
(`data/stage_a_search1..3.csv`, `data/stage_b_search1.json`,
`data/stage_b_search4.json`), because each changed a requirement or a bench.

### 3.1 The S1 charge decays through the reset switch

The charge from the S1 events lands on `vramp`, and it reaches `vrlo` only
through XRST (W 0.5 um). `vramp` decays in one slow mode, with
tau ~ R_on(XRST) x 4 pF + C/gm of the regulator: 19 ns typical and 29 ns at
ss/85 C/3.0 V for a 127 uW design. With an **ideal** vrlo source,
ramp_simple's switch alone leaves 0.048 LSB at TR at ss/85 C/3.0 V, at the
Analog_top timeline (90 ns from the last S1 to TR). That is 96 % of R5
before any regulator is added. Search 3 found designs fast enough to add
nothing, but every one of them failed phase margin in the ramp state.

### 3.2 The level error at 85 C

The systematic error at 85 C is the keep-alive (PMOS mirror) against the
shunt's mirror-set current (NMOS), corrected only by the loop gain. R9 was
first set at 1 LSB. That was inconsistent with the +-7.7 LSB ratiometric shift
already accepted, so it was re-derived from the overrun margin (2 LSB, s.1).

### 3.3 The timeline decision

Residual vs the TS3 -> TR gap, block model (`explore/gap.py`):

| gap | ideal source, ss/85 C/3.0 V | 33 uW hand design, ss/85 C/3.0 V | ~100-127 uW designs, ss/85 C/3.0 V |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 ns (Analog_top) | 0.048 LSB | 0.58 LSB | 0.11 LSB |
| 200 ns | 0.0007 | 0.12 | 0.003 |
| 300 ns | 0.0000 | 0.025 | 0.000 |

The user chose 300 ns (2026-10-10). It adds 200 ns per record against a
13.8 us conversion. It is a requirement on the controller and the board
timing. This block's benches apply it; Analog_top's own files are unchanged
(`macro.py` writes a run-local `tb_params.inc` with only the TR line changed).
With it, search 4 found 88 stage-A passers. The first in power x area order,
p55 (33.9 uW, 2075 um2), passed all ten stage-B cells.

### 3.4 Mismatch enters the search

p55 failed R10 in verification: sigma(vrlo) 1.52 LSB in 200 draws. Its NMOS
load and shunt fingers are L 0.5 x W 1 um, and with an OTA gain of 3 their
mismatch reaches the input almost undivided. A 60-draw scan of the passers
(`explore/mc_scan.py`) split cleanly by that length: L 1 um gave 0.66-0.84
LSB, most L 0.5 um designs 1.0-2.7 LSB.

Stage B now also requires two things at typical:
- a 60-draw sigma <= 0.85 LSB (R10 less about two standard errors);
- noise <= 0.85 x R11.

Search 5 used the same seed, so the same stage A. It rejected p55 (1.40),
p71 (0.92) and p98 (1.02 LSB). It selected **p195: 38.8 uW, 2942 um2,
0.75 LSB, 0.024 LSB noise**.

### 3.5 The S1 load model

The block benches model each cell as 40 fF precharged to its input and
switched onto `vramp` [A]. The real cells were measured in the macro
(`macro.py`, s.4.5). At the four mid-codes the signal-dependent charge is
8.7/18.9/30.0/24.9 fC, against 3.9/18.9/37.6/56.4 fC modelled. It saturates
near 30 fC because the comparator's input changes state.

What gates the model is the end-to-end check: the block bench's kick at
typical must not be smaller than the macro's. For p195 it is 1.5x larger
(0.003 vs 0.002 mV, both at the 1 uV resolution of the echo). For p55 it
was 2.24x [M].

## 4. Results

Record `runs/vrlo-ref/20261010T111343Z`, from `./scripts/sim.sh vrlo-ref
PHASES="size verify schematic macro"`. It has `pass: true` and no FAIL line.
The size phase confirms `vrlo_ref.spice` is what the recorded search selects.

The PVT grid is 5 corners x 0/27/85 C x 3.0/3.3/3.6 V. The spans are the
ideal bias at 0.69x/1.77x and the ramp current at 0.74x/1.62x (typical).
That makes 49 cells.

### 4.1 Operating point (49 cells)

- The tail, keep-alive, pair and g-diode are all in saturation. The worst
  margins are XMT +1.012 V (ss/0 C/3.0 V), XM1/XM2 +0.736 V (sf/85 C/3.0 V)
  and XM5 +0.423 V (ff/85 C/3.0 V).
- The shunt XMS keeps +0.367 V of margin (ss/85 C/3.0 V).
- vrlo - 5/33 AVDD lies between -1.60 and +6.95 mV. The worst, 1.19 LSB, is
  at ss/85 C/3.0 V: the 85 C mirror imbalance (s.3.2). At typical it is
  +0.87 mV.
- Supply current is 11.76 uA typical and 19.4 uA at most.

### 4.2 Transients (`tb_tran.spice`, 49 cells, forced end of ramp)

| Gate | Limit | Worst | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| start: vramp at T0 vs settled | 0.293 mV | 0.209 mV (0.036 LSB) | ss/0 C/3.6 V |
| kick: residual at TR, full-scale - empty cells | 0.293 mV | 0.128 mV (0.022 LSB) | ss/85 C/3.0 V |
| rec: reset level 1 us after the dump vs before | 0.293 mV | 0.124 mV (0.021 LSB) | ss/0 C/3.6 V |
| rep: vramp at TR, record 2 - record 1 | 0.293 mV | 0.044 mV (0.008 LSB) | ss/0 C/3.6 V |
| err: \|vrlo - 5/33 AVDD\| | 11.72 mV | 6.90 mV (1.18 LSB) | ss/85 C/3.0 V |

All 49 cells pass every gate. With an ideal source the switch's share at
TR is 0.0000 LSB at every cell (300 ns gap). The dump lifts vrlo to at most
1.29 V (ff/85 C/3.6 V) and it recovers within R4. Power in the reset state
is at most 64 uW over the cells.

**The real 13.8 us ramp** (`tb_tran.spice` without forcing, typical,
ss/0 C/3.0 V, ff/85 C/3.6 V) passes the same gates. The worst figures are
start 0.177 mV, kick 0.003 mV, rec 0.101 mV and rep 0.035 mV. The ramp
reaches 2.129 V before the reset (typical). The forced bench's rec and rep
agree with the real ramp within 0.014 mV at all three cells, so the 49-cell
figures stand for the real ramp.

## 5. Limits and open items

- **The bias is ideal.** IB = 1.37 uA (verified over 0.69x-1.77x) needs an
  output from a bias generator. That could be a new `bias_gen` sink or a
  slave of the ramp's diode. Not designed.
- **The timeline (R6) is a requirement on the controller.** It is not yet in
  Analog_top's `tb_params.inc` or in any RTL. Until it is, the macro benches
  there still release the ramp 100 ns after the last S1.
- **The level error is largest at 85 C.** At ss/85 C/3.0 V it is 6.95 mV
  systematic, 1.19 LSB of the 2 LSB allocation. With the MC sigma of
  0.78 LSB at typical, the worst systematic + 3 sigma is about 3.5 LSB:
  within the ~4 spare overrun counts, but without margin. The MC ran at
  typical only.
- **The divider's own mismatch is not in the Monte Carlo.** The
  `ppolyf_u_3k` model carries global variation only. 28 : 5 matched units
  set the ratio; their local mismatch adds to R10.
- **Ratiometric.** vrlo follows AVDD (s.1). AVDD noise and ripple reach vrlo
  x 0.15 below the loop bandwidth (`psr_*` metrics).
- **Not done:**
  - layout and PEX;
  - VACASK transient noise;
  - the macro at corners (the block benches cover PVT; the macro ran at
    typical only).
- **The reset switch.** At the 100 ns timeline, ramp_simple's XRST alone
  uses the whole R5 allocation at ss/85 C/3.0 V (s.3.1). That is a finding
  for ramp_simple: a wider XRST is the alternative to R6.
